# E-Commerce Customer Churn Analysis Using SQL

## 1. Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the major challenges faced by e-commerce businesses. Losing existing customers can negatively impact revenue, profitability, and long-term business growth.

This project focuses on analyzing customer behavior and identifying patterns that contribute to customer churn using **MySQL**. SQL queries are used to extract, clean, transform, and analyze customer data to generate meaningful business insights.

## 2. Business Problem

An e-commerce company is experiencing a decline in repeat customers. The management wants to understand why customers stop purchasing and identify customer segments that are more likely to churn.

The objective is to analyze customer purchasing behavior and provide data-driven recommendations to improve customer retention.

## 3. Project Objectives

- Identify customers who have stopped purchasing.
- Calculate the overall customer churn rate.
- Analyze customer purchase frequency.
- Identify high-value customers at risk of churn.
- Analyze churn across different customer segments.
- Understand the relationship between customer spending and churn.
- Provide recommendations to improve customer retention.

- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Average Purchase Frequency
- Repeat Customer Rate

**Churn Rate Formula:**

Churn Rate = (Number of Churned Customers / Total Customers) × 100

**Retention Rate Formula:**

Retention Rate = (Retained Customers / Total Customers at the Beginning of the Period) × 100

*Note: The churn definition and analysis period should be clearly established before calculating these KPIs.*
